Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy covers Ippon Autopost, the internal publishing tool of Ippon Daily.

What we access

When an Ippon Daily team member connects a social account, the tool receives an access token from that platform (Meta, Google/YouTube or TikTok) and basic profile information (account name and ID). It uses them only to upload and publish videos that the team has scheduled, and to show whether a post succeeded.

Where it is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only on the team's own computer, in a local file that is not shared or uploaded anywhere. We do not sell, share or transfer this data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting an account in the tool deletes its token. Access can also be revoked at any time from the platform's own settings (Facebook Business Integrations, Google Account permissions, TikTok Manage app permissions).

Google user data

Ippon Autopost's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
